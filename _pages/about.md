Hi! I am Terezia Slaninakova, Ph.D., a researcher at Masaryk University, Brno, Czech Republic. I build useful research production-ready applications at the intersection of machine learning, similarity searching, and bioinformatics. I am currently working at [cerit-sc](https://cerit-sc.cz/) department on scientific discovery services within the [eosc-cz](https://www.eosc.cz/) project. If you're working on interesting problems in vector search, RAG systems, scientific ML, or related areas, I'd love to connect!

My Ph.D. was dedicated to creating and improving a ML-based index for similarity search in high-dimensional data under the [GF23-07040K](https://starfos.tacr.cz/en/projekty/GF23-07040K#project-main) project co-shared with CAU in Kiel, Germany. The theoretical work ultimately lead to creating a similarity search system for finding proteins with similar structure in 500M+ proteins and protein domains, AlphaFind - [https://alphafind.ics.muni.cz](https://alphafind.ics.muni.cz/), used by researchers worldwide.  While on Ph.D., I attended three internships - in [Daisy lab](https://csj.cs.aau.dk/) under prof. Jensen in Aalborg, Denmark, in [Database Sysems and Data Mining group](https://www.uni-kiel.de/de/tf/forschen/institut-informatik/datenbanksysteme-und-data-mining) under prof. Kröger in CAU, Kiel, Germany, and, most recently, in [Greener group](https://www2.mrc-lmb.cam.ac.uk/groups/greener/members/) at MRC-LMB in Cambridge, UK. I also colaborated with David Sehnal from CEITEC, MU on [MolViewStories](https://molstar.org/mol-view-stories/).

For my M.S.c, I studied Artificial intelligence and Data processing, while working on projects at [CERIT-SC](https://www.cerit-sc.cz/), where I created ML pipelines for life tissue and remote sensing data. For my thesis, I [created a prototype of an ML-based index for metric spaces](https://is.muni.cz/th/f274m/).

I got my B.S.c-equivalent in Computer Science in Brno University of Technology in 2018, in my final thesis I focused on [generative AI applied to sound](https://www.vut.cz/www_base/zav_prace_soubor_verejne.php?file_id=181308) in [Speech@FIT lab](https://speech.fit.vut.cz). While studying I also worked at [Y Soft](ysoft.com) and [Adacta](https://adactainsurance.com) on creating automated quality assurance pipelines.

## Core Expertise

- **ML Systems:** PyTorch, embedding models, data processing pipelines, evaluation frameworks

- **Infrastructure:** Kubernetes, Docker, batch processing, production deployment

- **Domains:** High-dimensional data, search engines, scientific data, protein structure analysis, molecular dynamics
